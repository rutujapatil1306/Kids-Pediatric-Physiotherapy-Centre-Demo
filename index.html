Kids Pediatric Physiotherapy Centre focuses on pediatric physiotherapy through individualized sessions and developmental support, in a calm, comfortable space made for children and their families.

We believe a gentle, child-friendly approach works best, and we work alongside parents so that progress continues beyond the clinic.
